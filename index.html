Mission Statement

We remember the pre-internet world and saw technology merge into our lives. There will never be a generation who knows a world without technology completely dominating society. The tragedy: We got it all wrong. The promise of technology fortold an age of objective information forged by interfacing the world through direct-democracy. Instead, we are now dominated by the will of mega-corperation algorithm distraction and disinformation. As technology develops, technological literacy declines.

Our mission is to provide computer science education, software and infrustructure. Our company values curiosity, wisdom, and growth. We believe in horizontal, grassroots coordination and that customer satisfaction, open source advocacy, computer science education, and software security are the cornerstones we'll use to help. Each tenant supports and is supported by the others:

The Four Cornerstones:

  • Customer Satisfaction:
    • Although the customer is not always right, we will inform you of the solutions you didn’t know you needed. Please, let us convince you. Most people don’t realize the options available in an increasingly monopolized market, or have no idea of more secure, free open source options. We can update you on the current options of this ever complexifying and changing world to the current standard.
  • Open Source Advocacy:
    • Most available options are purposefully obfuscated in order to force you to pay more, granting you an inferior product. Customer satifaction, technology awaredness, and software security all granted by open sourcing software when we can all read the source code. Noone should have to pay simply to use a computer, an image processor, a word processor, or a coding IDE. Open source invites us to build and use things are commonly owned by the people, built to last and every single person is invited to publish their chapter in history.
  • Computer Science Education:
    • Computer Science now interfaces every other pursuit of knowledge. In the age of information, computer science has become the single most important field of study, required to advance in any other field. A person could live a thousand lifetimes studying computer science and not learn everything in the field. We can help you in that journey.
  • Software Security:
    • When the entire software development community has their collective eyes on the same sourcecode, security is assured. Even when vulnerabilities are discovered, solutions are both more comprehensive and rolled out faster. That is the difference between proprietary and open source software. Which would you trust more: software design a team of fifty developers who are constantly replaced, or the entire developer community who are familiar with their trusty tools they’ve known and maintained their whole lives. Security doesn’t end by choosing the correct stack, of course. Proper configuration and administration of infrustructure, software and firewalls. We will leave you peace of mind by educating.

In this world of distraction, talk is cheap. Computer science is the study of languages that actually produce automated and leveraged actions. Here is a breakdown of how our time is spent on action:

  • One-on-one Client Interaction:
    • When you are our client, email us any time and we will get back to you within a few hours. If you need to talk over the phone or video chat, we can schedule a meeting. When you are our client, you are our first priority.
  • Software Design:
    • Most weekdays involves building solutions for our clients. This includes software development but also includes UI/UX and graphic design. We are artists are heart, and computer science is the newest artistic medium yet to be realized as one.
    • Fridays are devoted to opensource projects. Supporting our small business with patronage means you are supporting opensource. Help us make our mark on the world.
  • Education
    • Every week we research what is happening in the open source community. We love learning about new APIs or libraries individual developers have created as their contribution to the world.
    • We are constantly grinding FreeCodeCamp and CodeWars to improve our game. Technique is everything.
    • We are in constant communication with our clients, relaying what we’ve learned, what options are available as solutions, and teaching scheduled computer science basics classes.

We are a small business with large ambitions. Join our community, your patronage is greatly appreciated.
